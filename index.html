Mission

A student-led space at URI to explore quantum computing, communication, and sensing through talks, hands-on workshops, and projects.

Learn together

Short talks, guided study, and friendly explanations—no prior background required.

Build real things

Hands-on projects, demos, and collaboration—ship a portfolio piece with a team.

Make it welcoming

Pairing, office hours, and community norms that help everyone participate.

A space to learn, experiment, and connect with others interested in quantum technologies.
