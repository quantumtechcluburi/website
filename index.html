Mission

QTC@URI brings together people who are curious about the rapidly evolving quantum field. We promote interdisciplinary learning, collaboration, professional growth, and greater awareness of how quantum technologies may shape science, business, and society.

Education

Learn quantum computing, quantum information science, and related technologies through introductory and technical workshops. No prior experience required.

Hands-on projects

Build practical skills through demos, programming challenges, hackathons, and collaborative projects designed to make quantum accessible to all students.

Community

Connect across engineering, computer science, physics, mathematics, chemistry, business, and other disciplines in a welcoming interdisciplinary STEM community.

Speakers & careers

Hear from faculty, researchers, and industry professionals. Discover internships, research positions, educational programs, and career opportunities.
